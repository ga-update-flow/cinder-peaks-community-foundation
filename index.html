Who we are

A permanent source of support for the high country

Cinder Peaks Community Foundation connects people who care with causes that matter, building lasting resources so that every community in northern Arizona can thrive for generations.

Founded in 1987 by Flagstaff families who wanted to keep charitable dollars working close to home, the Foundation now stewards 310 charitable funds and serves a region larger than many states, from the Grand Canyon and the Navajo Nation to the Verde Valley and the White Mountains.

Our funding priorities

Focus areas

Our grantmaking is organized around six community fields of interest and four donor-built community funds.

  • Health & Human Services

    Safe housing, enough food, and access to care for every household in the high country.

  • Civic & Economic Opportunity

    Good jobs, financial stability, and a strong civic voice for every community.

  • Education & Youth

    Strong pathways from kindergarten through college and career.

  • Environment & Land Stewardship

    Healthy forests, clean water, and resilient landscapes.

  • Arts & Culture

    Creative expression that connects people and carries heritage forward.

  • Animal Welfare

    Humane care for companion animals and protection for native wildlife.

  • Early Childhood

    A healthy start for every infant and toddler.

  • Indigenous Communities

    Native-led solutions grounded in language, land, and culture.

  • LGBTQ+ Communities

    Safety, health, and belonging for LGBTQ+ Arizonans.

  • Black Futures

    Investing in Black-led organizations and Black community wealth.

Applying for a grant

Three steps to apply

  1. Find the right program

    Review program pages and confirm eligibility with the Grant Eligibility Checklist.

  2. Download the guidelines

    Every program has a PDF guideline or RFP plus application templates in the document library.

    Follow the program's application instructions. Questions? Call (928) 555-0147.
