Health & Human Services

Safe housing, enough food, and access to care for every household in the high country.

Why it matters

Distance is a health issue in northern Arizona. Many families live an hour or more from a grocery store, pharmacy, or behavioral health provider, and housing costs in Flagstaff and Sedona have pushed working families farther from services. We invest in organizations that close those gaps.

Our goals

  • Increase the supply of affordable and supportive housing and prevent homelessness
  • Reduce food insecurity, especially in rural communities and on tribal lands
  • Expand access to primary, dental, and behavioral health care
  • Help older adults age safely in their own homes and communities
  • Strengthen services for survivors of domestic and sexual violence

Populations served

  • Low-income households
  • Older adults
  • People experiencing homelessness
  • Rural residents
  • Survivors of violence
