Focus Areas

Focus Areas

Our funding priorities reflect what we hear from communities across northern Arizona. Each focus area has goals, priority populations, and related grant programs.

Community Impact Fields of Interest

Six fields of interest guide our two Community Impact Grant cycles. Health and economic fields are funded each spring; education, arts, environment, and animal welfare are funded each fall.

Health & Human Services

Safe housing, enough food, and access to care for every household in the high country.

Grant programs: Community Impact Grants: Spring Cycle; High Country Rapid Response & Resilience Fund

Civic & Economic Opportunity

Good jobs, financial stability, and a strong civic voice for every community.

Grant programs: Community Impact Grants: Spring Cycle; Stronger Nonprofits Capacity Grants

Education & Youth

Strong pathways from kindergarten through college and career.

Grant programs: Community Impact Grants: Fall Cycle

Environment & Land Stewardship

Healthy forests, clean water, and resilient landscapes.

Grant programs: Community Impact Grants: Fall Cycle; High Country Rapid Response & Resilience Fund

Arts & Culture

Creative expression that connects people and carries heritage forward.

Grant programs: Community Impact Grants: Fall Cycle

Animal Welfare

Humane care for companion animals and protection for native wildlife.

Grant programs: Community Impact Grants: Fall Cycle

Community Funds & Initiatives

Four donor-built funds focus on communities and life stages where targeted investment makes an outsized difference. Each has its own advisory structure and application.

Early Childhood

A healthy start for every infant and toddler.

Grant programs: First Steps Infant & Toddler Fund

Indigenous Communities

Native-led solutions grounded in language, land, and culture.

Grant programs: Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund

LGBTQ+ Communities

Safety, health, and belonging for LGBTQ+ Arizonans.

Grant programs: Prism Fund for LGBTQ+ Arizonans

Black Futures

Investing in Black-led organizations and Black community wealth.

Grant programs: Rising Roots Black Futures Fund

Cinder PeaksCommunity Foundation

Cinder Peaks Community Foundation connects people who care with causes that matter, building lasting resources so that every community in northern Arizona can thrive for generations.
