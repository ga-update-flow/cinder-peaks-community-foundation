Early Childhood

A healthy start for every infant and toddler.

Why it matters

The First Steps Infant & Toddler Fund was created in 2014 by a coalition of Flagstaff families to make the first three years of life a regional priority. Our 2026 research found just one licensed infant care slot for every five infants in the four-county region.

Our goals

  • Increase the number of high-quality infant and toddler care slots
  • Support young parents ages 24 and under
  • Support grandparents and kin caregivers raising children under three
  • Strengthen pay, training, and retention in the early childhood workforce
  • Expand access to prenatal and reproductive health services for young adults

Populations served

  • Infants and toddlers ages 0–3
  • Young parents ages 24 and under
  • Grandparents and kin caregivers
  • Early childhood educators
