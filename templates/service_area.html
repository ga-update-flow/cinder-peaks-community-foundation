Coconino County

Arizona's largest county and home to our Flagstaff office. Communities include Flagstaff, Williams, Page, Fredonia, Tusayan, and Tuba City, along with portions of the Navajo Nation and the Hopi, Havasupai, Hualapai, Kaibab Paiute, and San Juan Southern Paiute communities.

Yavapai County

The Verde Valley and central highlands, including Prescott, Prescott Valley, Chino Valley, Cottonwood, Sedona, Camp Verde, and the Yavapai-Apache Nation and Yavapai-Prescott Indian Tribe.

Navajo County

From the Navajo Nation and Hopi Reservation in the north to the Mogollon Rim, including Winslow, Holbrook, Snowflake, Show Low, Pinetop-Lakeside, and Kayenta, and lands of the White Mountain Apache Tribe.

Apache County

Arizona's eastern edge, including Chinle, Ganado, Window Rock, St. Johns, Springerville, and Eagar. Most of the county lies within the Navajo Nation.

Statewide funds

Three community funds accept applications from organizations anywhere in Arizona: the Prism Fund for LGBTQ+ Arizonans, the Rising Roots Black Futures Fund, and the Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund.
