General questions

Can my organization apply to more than one program in the same year?

Yes. You may apply for one Community Impact Grant per calendar year and also apply to any of the community funds, the Stronger Nonprofits Capacity Grants, and the Rapid Response Fund.

What time are deadlines?

All deadlines are 6:00 p.m. Mountain Standard Time. Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so this is the same as Pacific Daylight Time from March through November.

Do you fund organizations outside northern Arizona?

The Prism Fund, Rising Roots Black Futures Fund, and Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund accept applications from anywhere in Arizona. All other programs serve Coconino, Yavapai, Navajo, and Apache counties.

Do you make grants to individuals?

No. Scholarships are awarded through separate donor-established scholarship funds. Individuals affected by emergencies can find help through nonprofits supported by our Rapid Response Fund.

Can I get feedback if my application is declined?

Yes. Contact the program officer listed on the program page to schedule a feedback call.
