The application process

  1. Choose a program. Compare programs on the Grant Opportunities page and read the program's PDF guidelines or RFP.
  2. Confirm eligibility. Use the Grant Eligibility Checklist and read our eligibility and grant policies.
  3. Talk with a program officer. We encourage a short call before you apply, especially if you are a first-time applicant.
  4. Prepare your materials. Download the templates listed for the program and draft your answers offline.
  5. Submit by the deadline. All deadlines are 5:00 p.m. Mountain Standard Time (Arizona time).
  6. Review and decision. Staff may request more information or schedule a site visit. Every applicant receives a decision by email.

Single-stage and two-stage programs

Single-stage programs ask for a complete application by one deadline. Two-stage programs begin with a two-page letter of inquiry (LOI); applicants whose letters best fit the program are invited to submit a full application.

Using the CPCF Grant Portal

The CPCF Grant Portal is the Foundation's online application system. New applicants create an organization profile with the organization's legal name, address, executive director, and annual budget. Returning applicants sign in with their existing email address and confirm that their profile is current before starting a new application.

The portal saves your work automatically, so you can return to an application before the deadline. If you need help creating an account or resetting a password, contact our Grants Manager, Theo Lindqvist, at tlindqvist@cinderpeakscf.org or (928) 555-0147 ext. 119. Applicants who are unable to use the portal may request a paper or emailed application.

Common attachments

  • Current fiscal year operating budget approved by your board
  • Most recent financial statements (audit, review, or board-approved statements)
  • List of board members and their affiliations
  • Project budget for project and program grants, using the Grant Project Budget Template
  • Fiscal sponsor letter, if applicable, using the Fiscal Sponsor Letter Template

Language and accessibility

We accept letters of inquiry for the Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund in Indigenous languages and oral formats, and Rising Roots applicants may submit a video. If you need an accommodation for any program, contact us at least one week before the deadline.
