Competitive capacity-building grant (two-stage)

Stronger Nonprofits Capacity Grants

Letters of inquiry due November 13, 2026

Grants of $5,000 to $20,000 that help small and mid-sized northern Arizona nonprofits strengthen governance, finances, fundraising, technology, and leadership.

Funding
$5,000 to $20,000
LOI deadline
November 13, 2026
Full application
January 22, 2027
Geography
Coconino County, Yavapai County, Navajo County, Apache County

Prism Fund for LGBTQ+ Arizonans

Accepting applications through November 20, 2026

Grants of $5,000 to $15,000 to organizations across Arizona that advance the health, safety, and belonging of LGBTQ+ people, with special attention to rural communities.

Funding
$5,000 to $15,000
Application deadline
November 20, 2026
Geography
Statewide (Arizona)

Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund

Letters of inquiry due December 4, 2026

Grants of $5,000 to $25,000 for Native-led organizations, tribal programs, and schools that strengthen Indigenous language, culture, land, and community well-being in Arizona.

Funding
$5,000 to $25,000
LOI deadline
December 4, 2026
Full application
February 5, 2027
Geography
Statewide (Arizona), Priority: northern Arizona tribal lands and border communities

First Steps Infant & Toddler Fund

Rolling emergency grant

High Country Rapid Response & Resilience Fund

Open year-round

Fast grants of up to $10,000 for nonprofits, tribal programs, and public agencies responding to wildfire, flooding, severe winter storms, and other emergencies in northern Arizona.

Up to $10,000; most awards range from $2,500 to $7,500
Deadline
Rolling, reviewed year-round

Coconino County, Yavapai County, Navajo County, Apache County
