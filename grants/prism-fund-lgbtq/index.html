Prism Fund for LGBTQ+ Arizonans

Grants of $5,000 to $15,000 to organizations across Arizona that advance the health, safety, and belonging of LGBTQ+ people, with special attention to rural communities.

Accepting applications through November 20, 2026

Program description

The Prism Fund was established in 2009 by LGBTQ+ donors and allies who wanted LGBTQ+ people in rural Arizona to have access to the same support available in the state's largest cities. Today the fund accepts applications from organizations statewide.

The fund supports both established LGBTQ+ organizations and mainstream organizations that are building programs to serve LGBTQ+ community members. Applications are reviewed by a committee of fundholders and community members.

Focus and impact areas

LGBTQ+ Communities

  • Ending new HIV transmissions and supporting people living with HIV
  • Affirming physical, behavioral, and gender-affirming health care
  • Safe and supportive schools for LGBTQ+ students
  • Addressing discrimination in housing, employment, and public services
  • Reducing isolation among LGBTQ+ older adults and rural residents

Population served

  • LGBTQ+ youth and young adults
  • Transgender, nonbinary, and two-spirit people
  • LGBTQ+ older adults
  • People living with or at risk of HIV
  • LGBTQ+ residents of rural communities

Eligibility

Geographic eligibility

Applications are accepted from organizations serving communities anywhere in Arizona. Proposals serving rural counties receive additional consideration.

Who may apply

  • Tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3), a public agency, a federally recognized tribe or tribal program, or a project with a qualified fiscal sponsor
  • Serves LGBTQ+ people in Arizona
  • Has an anti-discrimination policy that includes sexual orientation and gender identity
  • One application per organization per year

What we do not fund

  • Individuals or scholarships
  • Religious activities
  • Capital campaigns or endowments
  • Lobbying or partisan political activity

How to apply

The Prism Fund uses a single-stage application submitted through the CPCF Grant Portal.

  1. Read the guidelines. Download the Prism Fund 2026–2027 Grant Guidelines.
  2. Prepare the application. Use the Community Impact Grant Application Worksheet to draft responses. Prism applicants also answer two fund-specific questions listed in the guidelines.
  3. Submit online. Submit the application and budget in the CPCF Grant Portal by 5:00 p.m. MST on November 20, 2026.
  4. Decision. The Prism Fund committee reviews applications in December. Applicants are notified by January 15, 2027.

General instructions for the CPCF Grant Portal are on the How to Apply page.

Review criteria

  • Leadership and involvement of LGBTQ+ people in the organization
  • Clarity of need and approach
  • Reach into rural or underserved communities
  • Feasibility and organizational capacity

Reporting requirements

Grantees submit a Community Impact Story Report at the end of the grant year.

  • A short narrative or photo story describing results
  • Number of people served
  • Permission for public sharing, with care for participant privacy and safety

Due: February 28, 2028.

Reporting form: Community Impact Story Report Form (PDF)
