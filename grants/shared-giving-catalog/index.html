Shared Giving Catalog

An abbreviated profile application that places northern Arizona nonprofits in the Foundation's 2027 Shared Giving Catalog, which is distributed to more than 300 donor-advised fundholders.

Profiles due December 11, 2026

Program description

The Shared Giving Catalog introduces Foundation fundholders to vetted nonprofits across the region. Each listed organization receives a one-page profile describing its work and a specific funding need. Fundholders recommend grants directly from their donor-advised funds.

Listing in the catalog is not a competitive grant. In 2026, 112 catalog organizations received $1.27 million in fundholder grants, with individual gifts ranging from $500 to $25,000.

The catalog is a good option for organizations that received a Community Impact Grant in 2026 and are not eligible to apply again until 2028.

Eligibility

Geographic eligibility

Organizations must be located in or primarily serve residents of Coconino, Yavapai, Navajo, or Apache counties.

Who may apply

  • Tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3), a public agency, or a tribal program
  • Located in or primarily serving residents of Coconino, Yavapai, Navajo, or Apache counties
  • In operation for at least two years
  • Has filed its most recent IRS Form 990, 990-EZ, or 990-N

Funding

Funding amount
Organizations list a funding need of $1,000 to $15,000; fundholder gifts vary
Type of support
Fundholder grants recommended from donor-advised funds

How to apply

The catalog profile is a short form that takes about two hours to complete.

  1. Download the profile form. Complete the Shared Giving Catalog Profile Form, including a 150-word organizational summary and one funding need.
  2. Gather a photo. Select one high-resolution photo of your work and confirm you have permission from anyone pictured.
  3. Submit. Upload the form and photo in the CPCF Grant Portal under "Shared Giving Catalog 2027" by 5:00 p.m. MST on December 11, 2026.
  4. Review and publication. Foundation staff confirm eligibility and may suggest edits. The catalog is published to fundholders on February 1, 2027.

General instructions for the CPCF Grant Portal are on the How to Apply page.

Reporting requirements

Organizations that receive fundholder grants send a thank-you acknowledgment to the Foundation, which is forwarded to the fundholder. No formal grant report is required.

Due: Within 30 days of receiving a fundholder grant.
