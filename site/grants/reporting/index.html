Grant Reporting Requirements

We keep reporting simple and proportional to the size of the grant. Reporting requirements and due dates for every program are listed below.

ProgramWhat to submitDueForm
Community Impact Grants: Spring Cycle Grantees submit a brief Community Impact Story Report at the end of the grant period. July 31, 2028 Community Impact Story Report Form
Community Impact Grants: Fall Cycle Grantees submit a brief Community Impact Story Report at the end of the grant period. January 31, 2029 Community Impact Story Report Form
First Steps Infant & Toddler Fund Grantees submit an interim progress report at six months and a final grant report at the end of each grant year. Interim report due January 15, 2028; final report due July 31, 2028 (and July 31, 2029 for two-year grants) Interim Progress Report Form
Final Grant Report Form
Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund Grantees submit a final grant report within 30 days of the end of the grant period. Reports may be written, recorded as audio or video, or shared in a conversation with the program officer. April 30, 2028 Final Grant Report Form
Prism Fund for LGBTQ+ Arizonans Grantees submit a Community Impact Story Report at the end of the grant year. February 28, 2028 Community Impact Story Report Form
Rising Roots Black Futures Fund Grantees submit a Community Impact Story Report at the end of the grant year. April 30, 2028 Community Impact Story Report Form
Stronger Nonprofits Capacity Grants Grantees submit a mid-year progress report and a final grant report, and participate in all four cohort sessions. Interim report due September 15, 2027; final report due March 31, 2028 Interim Progress Report Form
Final Grant Report Form
High Country Rapid Response & Resilience Fund Grantees submit a one-page Rapid Response Use of Funds Report within 60 days of spending the grant. Within 60 days after funds are spent Rapid Response Use of Funds Report
Shared Giving Catalog Organizations that receive fundholder grants send a thank-you acknowledgment to the Foundation, which is forwarded to the fundholder. No formal grant report is required. Within 30 days of receiving a fundholder grant Thank-you acknowledgment

Submitting a report

Upload reports in the CPCF Grant Portal under "My Grants," or email them to grants@cinderpeakscf.org. If you expect to miss a due date or need to change how grant funds are used, contact the Grants Manager before the due date.
