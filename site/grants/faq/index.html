Grant Seeker FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most often from applicants.

General questions

Can my organization apply to more than one program in the same year?

Yes. You may apply for one Community Impact Grant per calendar year and also apply to any of the community funds, the Stronger Nonprofits Capacity Grants, and the Rapid Response Fund.

What time are deadlines?

All deadlines are 5:00 p.m. Mountain Standard Time. Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so this is the same as Pacific Daylight Time from March through November.

Do you fund organizations outside northern Arizona?

The Prism Fund, Rising Roots Black Futures Fund, and Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund accept applications from anywhere in Arizona. All other programs serve Coconino, Yavapai, Navajo, and Apache counties.

Do you make grants to individuals?

No. Scholarships are awarded through separate donor-established scholarship funds. Individuals affected by emergencies can find help through nonprofits supported by our Rapid Response Fund.

Can I get feedback if my application is declined?

Yes. Contact the program officer listed on the program page to schedule a feedback call.

Community Impact Grants: Spring Cycle

We received a Fall 2026 Community Impact Grant. Can we apply in Spring 2027?

No. Organizations that received a Community Impact Grant in calendar year 2026 must wait until the 2028 cycles. You may still apply to the Foundation's other funds and to the Shared Giving Catalog.

Can a fiscally sponsored project apply?

Yes. Include a signed letter from your fiscal sponsor using the Fiscal Sponsor Letter Template. The award tier is based on the project's budget, not the sponsor's.

Full program details for Community Impact (Spring)

Community Impact Grants: Fall Cycle

Are public schools eligible?

Yes. Public school districts, charter schools, and Bureau of Indian Education schools may apply. A school's award tier is based on the budget of the program or school site, not the full district budget.

Can we apply to both the Fall Cycle and the Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund?

Yes. The Plateau Fund has a separate review process, and applying to it does not affect Community Impact eligibility.

Full program details for Community Impact (Fall)

First Steps Infant & Toddler Fund

Can we request two years of funding?

Yes. Two-year requests may be up to $40,000 per year. Second-year funding is released after the Foundation approves the first-year final report.

Do family child care homes qualify?

Licensed or certified family child care homes may apply through a fiscal sponsor or as part of a network led by an eligible organization.

Full program details for First Steps Fund

Plateau Indigenous Communities Fund

Can a chapter house apply directly?

Yes. Navajo Nation chapters and Hopi villages may apply directly or through a tribal program or Native-led nonprofit acting as fiscal sponsor.

Can the letter of inquiry be submitted in a Native language?

Yes. Letters and oral submissions are welcome in Diné bizaad, Hopilavayi, and other Indigenous languages. The Foundation will arrange translation.
