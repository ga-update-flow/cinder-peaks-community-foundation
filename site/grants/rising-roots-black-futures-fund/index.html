Rising Roots Black Futures Fund

Grants of $2,500 to $10,000 to Black-led organizations and programs across Arizona that build community wealth, preserve Black history and culture, and develop Black youth leadership.

Opens December 1, 2026

Program description

The Rising Roots Black Futures Fund was launched in 2021 by Black philanthropists in northern Arizona who wanted to increase investment in Black-led organizations and preserve the history of Black communities in the region, including the families who built Flagstaff's railroad and lumber industries.

Grants support Black-led nonprofits and grassroots groups statewide. First-time applicants and organizations with annual budgets under $250,000 are especially encouraged to apply.

Focus and impact areas

Black Futures

  • Community and economic development in Black communities
  • Black artistic expression and preservation of cultural heritage
  • Financial literacy, homeownership, and entrepreneurship
  • Black youth leadership and educational achievement

Population served

  • Black residents and families in Arizona
  • Black youth and students
  • Black entrepreneurs and small business owners
  • Black artists and historians

Eligibility

Geographic eligibility

Applications are accepted from organizations serving communities anywhere in Arizona.

Who may apply

  • Black-led 501(c)(3) organizations, or Black-led projects and grassroots groups with a qualified fiscal sponsor
  • Organizations not Black-led may apply for programs designed with and for Black communities, documented by a letter of partnership
  • Serves Black communities in Arizona
  • One application per organization per year

What we do not fund

  • Individuals or scholarships
  • Religious activities
  • Capital campaigns or endowments
  • Lobbying or partisan political activity

How to apply

Rising Roots uses a short, single-stage application. Applicants may submit written answers or a video of up to ten minutes.

  1. Read the RFP. Download the Rising Roots Black Futures Fund 2027 Request for Proposals.
  2. Prepare the application. Draft answers to the five application questions listed in the RFP, or record a video response.
  3. Submit. Submit through the CPCF Grant Portal, or email materials to grants@cinderpeakscf.org with the subject line "Rising Roots – [Organization Name]", by 5:00 p.m. MST on January 29, 2027.
  4. Decision. The Rising Roots Advisory Circle reviews applications in February. Applicants are notified by March 12, 2027.

General instructions for the CPCF Grant Portal are on the How to Apply page.

Review criteria

  • Black leadership and community accountability
  • Alignment with fund priorities
  • Potential for lasting benefit to Black communities
  • Feasibility of the plan and budget

Reporting requirements

Grantees submit a Community Impact Story Report at the end of the grant year.

  • A short narrative, photo, or video story describing results
  • Number of people served
  • Permission for public sharing

Due: April 30, 2028.

Reporting form: Community Impact Story Report Form (PDF)
