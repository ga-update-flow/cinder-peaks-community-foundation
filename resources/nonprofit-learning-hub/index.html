Nonprofit Learning Hub

The Nonprofit Learning Hub offers workshops, peer cohorts, and practical guides for executive directors, staff, and board members across northern Arizona. In 2025, 412 people attended 38 sessions. Hub programs pair with our Stronger Nonprofits Capacity Grants.

2026–2027 programs

  • Executive Director Peer Circle: monthly peer learning for nonprofit leaders, meeting in Flagstaff and online.
  • Board Essentials: a three-session series on governance, finance, and fundraising for board members.
  • Grant Writing Fundamentals: a two-part workshop offered before each Community Impact cycle.
  • On the Road: workshops hosted in Page, Chinle, Show Low, and Cottonwood.

To register, email learning@cinderpeakscf.org.
